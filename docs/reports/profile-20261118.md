## Reading

- Bus trip updates shrink only 2–3.4x in daytime if stored change-only, far less than the 10x first assumed: at peak about half of all stop predictions change every 30 s. A likely cause is that a delay change on a trip updates every remaining stop. Storing a per-trip delay as it changes may cut far more; not yet tested.
- Vehicle positions barely shrink (1.1–1.2x): buses move, so keep every point (bunching needs them).
- Train trip updates fit change-only well (6–27x).
