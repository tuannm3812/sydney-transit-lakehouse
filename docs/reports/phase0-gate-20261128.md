# Phase 0 seven-day capture gate

## Summary

> The gate **passed**: every one of the 12 feeds captured at least 99% of its scheduled polls over the seven days, with no gap longer than four poll intervals except 1 upstream outage on 10 feeds, allowed under D-037.

- **The lowest feed was `buses_tripupdates` at 99.84%** over the seven days (section 3.1).
- **The longest gap was 285 s** on `buses_vehiclepos` (section 3.1).
- **7 daily checks are in the window** (21 to 27 Nov), one per cron run at 00:05 UTC (section 3.2).
- **Incidents before the window are in the incident log.** TfNSW's 502 outage on 20 Nov restarted the clock (D-033, I-06).

## Scope and method

The collector polls TfNSW's GTFS-realtime feeds from AWS EC2 in Sydney and records every attempt in its manifest (D-007).

```flow
source | TfNSW Open Data | realtime and timetable feeds
raw | Collector | every attempt in the manifest
check | Capture check | 7-day gate, read-only
```

| Check | Command | Window |
|---|---|---|
| Seven-day gate | `checks capture --window 7d --gate` | 21 Nov 2026 00:28 to 28 Nov 2026 00:28 UTC |
| Daily checks | `checks capture --window 24h` (cron, 00:05 UTC) | one row per run |

The gate needs, for every enabled feed, capture of at least 99% of scheduled polls, no gap over four intervals, known freshness and full history for the window (D-022). A gap TfNSW caused (every attempt a 5xx or network error while the collector kept polling) is allowed up to 30 minutes a day and 2 outages a week (D-037). Out of scope: the tables built from the data (Phase 1).

## Findings

- **Every feed cleared 99% capture over the seven days; the lowest was bus trip updates at 99.84%** (20,128 of 20,160 polls; section 3.1). The other eleven feeds were between 99.88% and 100%.
- **One upstream outage, allowed under D-037.** TfNSW's slowdown on 23 Nov, 02:35 to 02:52 UTC (read timeouts and 504s, I-18), shows on 10 feeds as one outage of 2.7 to 9.7 minutes with gaps of 164 to 285 s. The collector kept polling and recorded every attempt, so it counts as upstream, within 30 minutes a day and 2 outages a week. Service alerts and the static timetable had no outage.
- **Overnight freezes are exempt under D-055.** Both ferry feeds repeated an empty snapshot for about 2.3 h on 25 Nov while no ferries ran (I-22), and light rail trip updates repeated one snapshot for 27 min overnight. They show as stuck warnings, not failures.
- **Freshness held.** p95 freshness was 5 to 20 s on the realtime feeds and 85 s on service alerts (section 3.1).
- **The collector ran without a restart through the whole window.** Its process has run 9a8185f since 21 Nov 11:38 UTC; later deploys rebuilt the images only, so the checks ran on 6625e13 from 26 Nov 02:45 UTC while collection was untouched.

## Limits

- **Capture is measured against our own schedule.** It shows we asked on time and got an answer, not that TfNSW's data was complete or correct.
- **Upstream outages outside the window are not shown here.** See docs/10_incidents.md.
