## Verdict

The collector, capture checks, S3 offload, compaction, and D-038/D-047 gold fixes are disciplined and close most of the 18 Nov Codex review items, with the 7-day gate infrastructure sound aside from minor reporting and backoff edge cases. However, three correctness bugs in the gold layer and one state-wipe bug in the light worker directly alter ghost-trip, bunching, and stop-arrival judgements: `fct_trip_outcome` lets `ran` and `cancelled` bypass capture coverage, `stops_ahead` ignores D-028's trip-delay offset, `fct_headway` suppresses bunched pairs when vehicles overtake or tie on timestamp, and `FeedDiffer` wipes its prior-snapshot state on any unreadable or corrupt file. Fix those four defects, plus open `I-20`/`I-21`/`O-04`, before running the post-gate S3 backfill (`D-040`) and Phase 3 gate (`D-046`).

## Decisions to revisit

- **D-035 & D-047 (Gold trip judging and stops-to-go):** Revisit how `fct_trip_outcome` enforces coverage (`D-035` item 4) so `ran` and `cancelled` do not bypass `tu_covered` and skew `mart_route_honesty`, and clarify `D-047` rule (2) so `stops_ahead` explicitly reconstructs predictions relative to `trip_delay_s` / `current_delay_s` (from `D-028`).
- **D-045 (Bunching rule):** Revisit `D-045` (2) / `fct_headway` to specify how overtaken pairs (`scheduled_headway_s < 0`) are counted instead of silently dropping them from `fct_bunching_event` and `mart_bunching_hotspot`.
- **D-046 & D-026 (Phase 3 gate on pruned history and low-confidence school routes):** Clarify how `gold handcheck` (`D-046`) accesses raw files older than 3 days after `offload prune --keep 3d` (`D-026`) deletes local raw snapshots, and record a rule for how `low`-confidence trips on school/special routes with zero seen route siblings (`R3-01` flagged 6 of 20 on Mon 23 Nov) are treated under the 18-of-20 bar.
- **D-040 & D-027 (Memory cap and open backfill blockers):** `docker-compose.yml:69,93` still sets `mem_limit: 900m` for `worker` and `gold` (whereas `D-040` and `I-19` cite a `700 MB` cap), `I-20` (unbounded snapshot growth during `replay`) and `I-21` (windowed gold build error) are still open, and a full-day `gold build` has not yet been measured under the host's `GOLD_MEMORY_LIMIT=300MB` (only 2 hours in `R2-01`; `R3-01` peaked at 9.2 GB with `8GB` allowed).
