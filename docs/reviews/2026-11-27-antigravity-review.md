## Verdict

All six findings from [2026-11-25-antigravity-review-2.md](../blob/c068e5714c0e56cad91168f48068124888f7d65c/docs/reviews/2026-11-25-antigravity-review-2.md) are closed and hold on `HEAD`, and the new Redpanda + Spark benchmark in `06059d32..HEAD` (`c068e57`, `D-056`) faithfully reuses the worker's own `FeedDiffer` and `snapshot_from_bytes` so row semantics stay aligned with the light worker (`ruff check .`, `ruff format --check .`, and 63 tests in `test_bench.py`, `test_worker.py`, `test_replay.py`, and `test_checks.py` pass; gold/handcheck tests were blocked in the local `.venv` by missing `dbt` and `pytz`). No critical (`C`) bugs were introduced, though three important (`B`) operational/benchmark-fidelity issues in the new `bench` stack should be fixed before the `D-041` benchmark day: unpinned `protobuf` across `Dockerfile` and `Dockerfile.bench` can trip `run_day.sh`'s preflight, `Sink.maintain()` compacts entire `(obs_date, mode)` partitions rather than closed hours (`D-032`), and `run_day.sh` swallows `bench-stream` failures and hits open incident `I-20` on Run C.
