### Decisions to revisit

- **D-059 (`empty_endings` exemption in `dq_trip_left.sql:56-57` and `gate.py:589-598`):** Exempting every readable snapshot with `entities = 0` from `trip_left:burst` (`mass_endings`) applies across *all* modes and *all* hours, not just Sydney Trains' ~01:00 Sydney reset. A daytime empty-snapshot glitch on buses, metro, ferries, or light rail that ends 100% of active trips in peak hour would only emit a `trip_left:empty` warning and still pass `gold gate`. Narrow the exemption to `mode = 'train'` (or overnight hours).
- **D-060 (`gold build --day` scope vs. `mart_bunching_hotspot`, `views.py`, and `handcheck`):** Building gold one day at a time left `mart_bunching_hotspot` coupled to windowed `stg_service_day` (dropping history >2 days) and left `views.py` with only a lower `obs_date` bound (breaking multi-day `--day` backfill memory bounds). Both `mart_bunching_hotspot` and `gold handcheck` need explicit support for per-day `transit.duckdb` state.
- **D-059 (`stg_rt_run` from `trip_status` only, and duplicate-stop mapping):** `stg_rt_run` ignores `vehicle_position`, dropping VP-only train runs in `stg_rt_vehicle_position`. Also, mapping repeated `stop_id` calls in a pattern to `min(stop_sequence)` maps the final arrival of loop trains returning to their origin (e.g., City Circle at Central) onto `stop_sequence = 1` (`at_origin`).
- **D-050 (numbering gap in `docs/3_decisions.md:55-56`):** Still jumps from `D-049` to `D-051` (carried over from previous reviews).
