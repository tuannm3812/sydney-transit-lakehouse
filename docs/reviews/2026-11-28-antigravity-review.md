## Review text

### Verdict
The changes in `8200f82..4cc6b84` fix real build and rendering problems (renaming `static/replay/` to `static/replay-map/`, aggregating `bunching_hotspots` per stop/route/day type, and adding `gold publish --from/--to`) without touching the silver layer or gold dbt models. However, `gold publish --from/--to` leaves `bunching_hotspots`, `bunching_by_hour`, and `quality` unwindowed while `I-21` forces builds to start a day early, `index.md`'s new `worst_route` query merges routes sharing a short name and operator across regions/modes, and `about.md` converts a UTC `TIMESTAMPTZ` to session-local time before labelling it `(UTC)`. All four findings from the 27 Nov review still hold on their original pages.

### Decisions to revisit
- **D-045 (4) & D-014 (`dashboard/static/replay-map/index.html`, `dashboard/pages/bunching.md`):** Switching basemaps from CARTO to `https://tile.openstreetmap.org/{z}/{x}/{y}.png` works for local builds, but as [docs/12_phase3.md](../../docs/12_phase3.md#L62) notes, OpenStreetMap's tile servers disallow heavy public-site traffic. Revisit tile hosting (a free-tier key or self-hosted PMTiles) before making the dashboard public (D-014).
- **D-045 (5) & `I-21` (`src/transit_lakehouse/gold/publish.py`):** `I-21` remains open in [docs/10_incidents.md](../../docs/10_incidents.md#L37), and `dashboard/README.md` / `quality.md` now bake the "start `gold build` a day early" workaround into the site workflow, leaking extra days into `/bunching` and `/quality`. Resolve `I-21` and make `publish --from/--to` window all public product tables before the S3 backfill (D-040) and launch.
- **D-050 (missing id) & `I-20`, `O-04`:** Carried over from the 27 Nov review: `D-050` is still absent from [docs/3_decisions.md](../../docs/3_decisions.md#L55-L56), and `I-20` (replay compaction/expiry) and `O-04` (Trains start dates and light rail stop arrivals) remain open in [docs/10_incidents.md](../../docs/10_incidents.md#L36-L45).
